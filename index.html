How European Spots Work

Domestic league standings determine which clubs qualify for UEFA's prestigious continental competitions based on each country's 5-year UEFA coefficient ranking.

UEFA Champions League

The crown jewel of club football. The top 4 clubs from England, Spain, Italy, and Germany qualify directly for the 36-team Swiss-model league phase.
