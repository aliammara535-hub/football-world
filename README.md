# ⚽ EuroLeagues Hub - European Football Guide

A modern, responsive, and interactive web application delivering comprehensive insights, historical records, and tactical profiles for Europe's most famous domestic football championships.

🌐 **Repository**: [https://github.com/aliammara535-hub/football-world](https://github.com/aliammara535-hub/football-world)

---

## 🏆 Featured Leagues

- 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 **Premier League (England)** - The world's most-watched domestic sports league.
- 🇪🇸 **La Liga (Spain)** - Home of El Clásico and European continental dominance.
- 🇮🇹 **Serie A (Italy)** - The historic bastion of tactical mastery and passionate tifosi.
- 🇩🇪 **Bundesliga (Germany)** - High-pressing football under the famous 50+1 fan ownership model.
- 🇫🇷 **Ligue 1 (France)** - The talent academy of global superstars.
- 🇳🇱 **Eredivisie (Netherlands)** - The birthplace of Total Football and Cruyffian philosophy.
- 🇵🇹 **Primeira Liga (Portugal)** - The gateway for South American talent and Os Três Grandes.

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Live Search & Filter**: Instant filtering by country and real-time search across leagues, clubs, and champions.
- **Detailed League Dossier**: Expandable modal with competition formats, trophy specifications, famous derbies, and milestone records.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix**: Full comparison of team sizes, European spots, relegation formats, and historical top scorers.
- **UEFA European Qualification Guide**: Explains qualifying berths for the UEFA Champions League, Europa League, and Conference League.
- **Interactive Trivia Quiz**: Test football knowledge with instant scoring and contextual trivia facts.
- **Dark / Light Mode**: Seamless theme toggle with state preserved via `localStorage`.
- **Pure Web Technologies**: Built with zero build steps using clean HTML5, CSS3, and modern Vanilla JavaScript.
